# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Discuss code, not people. Assume the other person is trying to help.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discrimination of any kind, publishing someone's private information, and
deliberately derailing a discussion. Logs and reports posted in issues must not show other people's
files or data.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space that represents this project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/thalesholleben/restic-twin/security/advisories/new),
which is private, or by contacting [@thalesholleben](https://github.com/thalesholleben). Reports are
handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, and block accounts, when
this document is broken.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), kept short
on purpose.
